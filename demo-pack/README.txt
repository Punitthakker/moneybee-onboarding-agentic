MoneyBee x Botim onboarding - demo pack
========================================

Synthetic documents for three made-up merchants. Every page is marked SAMPLE / NOT VALID.
Upload a whole folder: in the merchant portal (drop all files at once) or in the ops console (New case).

A - Golden Palm Restaurant (clean, low risk)
  Company: Golden Palm Restaurant LLC - choose "LLC (mainland)"
  Files: 12
  What the agents show: All 11 required documents recognised, fields extracted, owners matched to their IDs, 2 beneficial owners (60% and 40%), screening clear, risk LOW. Approve, then complete to mint the onboarding token.

B - Blue Dhow Logistics (layered ownership)
  Company: Blue Dhow Logistics LLC - choose "LLC (mainland)"
  Files: 16
  What the agents show: 70% is held through two holding companies. The agents trace the chain: Huda Al Example (30%) and Yousef Al Sample (28% through Coral Bay) are beneficial owners; Noura Demo and Karim Testwood (21% each) fall below the 25% threshold. See the Ownership tab.

C - Redline General Trading (sanctions hit)
  Company: Redline General Trading LLC - choose "LLC (mainland)"
  Files: 11
  What the agents show: The sole owner matches an entry on the MOCK sanctions list (a made-up name). Rating REFER, a second reviewer is required, and the memo explains why. Shows that the agents recommend but never approve.

Portal answers that match these packs: VAT registered = Yes, corporate shareholders = No (Yes for B), chamber member = No.
Real documents also upload fine. Without a live model the agents cannot read them, so they are sorted by file name
or the merchant picks the type ("What is this document?") and a reviewer confirms. Documents are optional at every step.
